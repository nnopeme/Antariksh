A Letter to My Kiddo

Hi my kiddo,

It's been six months since I first spoke to you, and in that time, you've become one of the most important people in my life...

Out of 8 billion smiles in this world, yours is the only one that brings me to life...

Sure, we have our fights and disagreements, but you're so precious to me...

I could never fully express what you truly mean to me...

Remember when you told me to thank my stars for meeting you?...

Yours, in all ways, Always.
